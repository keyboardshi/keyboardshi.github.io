Aloha! We are Keyboards Hawaii, a local group of custom keyboard enthusiasts primarily gathered around the island of Oahu.

Come chat with the community over at Discord @[dsc.gg/kbhi](https://dsc.gg/kbhi) or check us out on Instagram [@keyboardshawaii](https://www.instagram.com/keyboardshawaii/)  and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@keyboardshawaii).

For meetup or sponsorship inquiries, you may reach us at [keyboardshi@gmail.com](mailto:keyboardshi@gmail.com).


# Meetups

And we're back for our annual end-of-year meetup! This year we will be meeting on Saturday, December 5th at a new location! We'll be in [Hawaii Coworking](https://hawaiicoworking.co/) over in Waikiki, located in [Eaton Square](https://www.eatonsquarehi.com/).

Eaton Square has a secure parking garage that we will be able to use, but street parking is available as well.

So come hang out, catch up with old friends, and check out the latest keyboard builds!
